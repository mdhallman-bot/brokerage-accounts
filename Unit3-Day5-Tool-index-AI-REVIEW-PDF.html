Step 0

Build financial safety first

Emergency fund: money set aside for unexpected expenses. It should be safe and easy to access.

You are 16 and have $500 from a summer job. Why is keeping some money in an emergency fund recommended before investing all $500?

Investments always lose money in the short term.
You may need cash soon, and selling an investment during a market drop could lock in a loss.
Emergency funds always earn more than investments.

Liquidity = how quickly/easily something can become usable cash without a major loss in value. Which is generally more liquid?

Step 1

What is a brokerage?

Brokerage: a company that provides an account and the tools/access needed to buy, sell, and hold investments.

You generally do not send your money directly to a company every time you want to buy a share. A brokerage connects investors to markets and keeps track of the investments held in the account.

ACCOUNT = container
Brokerage account, 401(k), IRA/Roth IRA
INVESTMENT = contents
Stock, bond, ETF, mutual fund

Because you are under 18, an adult generally needs to be involved. The exact teen/custodial structure depends on the brokerage.
